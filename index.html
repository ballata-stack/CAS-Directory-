Quick guide

How to use the directory

Choose your role for a short guide to viewing and submitting information.

T

For teachers

Update your activity in a few minutes

Requests are reviewed by an administrator before the public directory changes.

1

Sign in

Use your school Google account. The site will recognize your teacher access automatically.

2

Open “Submit update”

Choose what you need to change: meeting details, advisor, student officer, or the activity itself.

3

Select the activity

Choose the club or activity from the dropdown. For a new activity, enter its complete name and category.

4

Enter the information

Add the meeting location, day, and time. For people, select a name from the school list.

Meeting LocationRoom 2097DayWednesdayTimeLunch
5

Add student officers

Select the student and position. If the name or position is missing, choose the manual or “Others” option.

Enter manual names as Last Name, First Name. If a second President is approved, both appear as Co-President.

6

Send and track the request

Add an optional note, select “Send for approval,” then check “My recent requests” for its status.

S

For students

Find an activity or submit your role

You can browse the directory and request a correction to your own leadership position.

1

Sign in

Use your school Google account. Your student access is assigned from the school list.

2

Find an activity

Use Directory search for an activity, advisor, student, or position. Use the filters to show only a category or student officers.

3

View complete details

Select an activity to see its advisors, student leadership, meeting location, day, and time.

4

Submit your position

Open “Submit a request,” select “Add or update my position,” choose the activity, and select your position.

5

Use “Others” when needed

If your exact position is not listed, choose “Others” and type the full position name.

Your name comes from your signed-in school account, so another student cannot submit a position in your name.

6

Wait for approval

Your request stays Pending until an administrator reviews it. Approved changes then appear in the directory.
